Inside a scene

Built from open data,
put together on your device.

If a source doesn’t respond, the view tells you in plain words and shows what it used instead.

Terrain at true scale

Elevation data shapes the ground, with distant ridgelines and coastlines out to the horizon. Heights are never exaggerated.

Satellite imagery

Aerial imagery is draped over the terrain, and land-cover data decides where there is forest, field, sand or water.

Buildings and streets

Mapped footprints become buildings with roof shapes and facades in the local style, along with roads, rail, bridges, quays and harbour boats.

Recognisable landmarks

Well-known sights get their own models, and monuments that are mapped in detail are built from their real parts.

Local vegetation

Trees and ground cover follow the local climate and canopy height, so dry places stay dry and forests look like forests.

Current weather

Cloud, rain, coastal fog and thunderstorms with lightning, based on the conditions at that place right now.

Settings

  • Render quality Fast, Balanced, High or Ultra, with automatic adjustment to keep the frame rate smooth.
  • Appearance Night, Day or system theme, and an accent colour that can follow the sun.
  • Accessibility Reduce motion, solid panels, higher contrast and screen-reader announcements.
  • Storage and units Choose how much to cache, and metric or imperial units.

About this site

This site shows the app using real captures from the project. Place previews, favourites, photo downloads and shareable preview links all work in your browser.

The app itself adds place search, on-device 3D generation, free movement, drone flight, live weather, calendar controls and offline downloads. This website doesn’t run the Godot app.

Scene data: AWS Terrain Tiles; Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community; © OpenStreetMap contributors; CHELSA V2.1. Map screens: OpenFreeMap and © OpenMapTiles. Landmark content in the app: Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons.

Favourites are stored in this browser. No account or sign-up is needed.
